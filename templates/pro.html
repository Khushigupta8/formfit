Questions

Do the free calculators stay free?

Yes. Every calculator, workout plan and guide stays free. Pro adds things that need your own data: a plan built for you, progress you can come back to, and no ads.

How do I cancel?

On your account page, in one click. You won't be charged again, and Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for.

Is the plan medical advice?

No. It uses the same formulas as our calculators to give you a sensible starting point. If you have a medical condition, are pregnant, or are under 18, talk to a doctor first. See our medical disclaimer.

What happens to my data?

Your plans and progress entries are stored in your account so you can see them on any device. You can delete any entry, or ask us to delete your account. Details are in our privacy policy.

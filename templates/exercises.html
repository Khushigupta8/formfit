How to use the library

Most well-built programmes are made of a handful of compound lifts (a squat, a hinge, a horizontal and vertical push, a horizontal and vertical pull) plus a few accessories. Learn those patterns first. Each page shows how to set up, the cues coaches use, and the mistakes that cost lifters the most progress.

If you want the exercises arranged into a weekly routine, every one of them appears in at least one of our workout plans.
